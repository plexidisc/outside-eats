quality you can taste

Downtown San Mateo features a bustling restaurant scene and thriving retail community all tucked into just five square blocks. You can’t possibly cover every dining and boutique experience represented Downtown in one visit, and that’s a good thing – because there’s so much here to keep you coming back.

The diversity of our Downtown entertainment and shopping options is unique for a city of our size. And so is the quality – which you can taste every night at our Michelin-starred eateries.
