Restaurant Feature
Pausa Bar & Cookery
Italian · San Mateo · $$$
Michelin Bib Gourmand Wine Spectator Award 2018– Gambero Rosso International

When I sat down with Chef-Owner Andrea Giuliani at Pausa Bar & Cookery, I asked him the question every first-time visitor should ask: what do I order? Without hesitation, he said the charcuterie board and the margherita pizza — and after trying both, I completely understand why.

Pausa — Italian for "pause" — is Andrea's invitation to step away from the pace of daily life and settle into something genuinely special. Born in the Veneto region of Italy and cooking professionally for more than 30 years, Andrea opened Pausa on E. 4th Avenue in San Mateo after leading kitchens at some of the Bay Area's best Italian restaurants. Every element of the menu — the house-made pasta, the artisan bread, the fresh mozzarella, the hand-crafted desserts — reflects that depth of experience.

The charcuterie board is a window into Andrea's craft: all house-cured salumi, made entirely from scratch in-house. The margherita pizza, meanwhile, earned Pausa a spot in the Michelin Guide's roundup of the best pizzas in and around San Francisco — a recognition that's hard to argue with once you've had a slice. These are the two dishes Andrea would put in front of any first-timer, and they make an ideal introduction to everything the restaurant does well.

Pausa has also been recognised by the San Mateo Chamber of Commerce as the best Italian restaurant in the San Mateo area — a local nod that sits alongside its Michelin Bib Gourmand designation (held for multiple consecutive years) and Wine Spectator's Award of Excellence every year since 2018. Whether you're there for Spritz Hour in the outdoor parklet, a date night in the dining room, or a seat at the Chef's Table, the kitchen delivers.
